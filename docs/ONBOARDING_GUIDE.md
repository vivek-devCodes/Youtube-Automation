# User guide (no technical knowledge needed)

## First visit
1. Open the app and **create your account**. The first account is the administrator.
2. The **setup wizard** walks you through 10 steps:
   1. **Welcome**: what the studio does and doesn't promise.
   2. **YouTube channel**: if you have one, click **Connect with Google** and approve. If not, follow the on-screen steps to create one on YouTube, then connect it. Not ready? Click **Create a sandbox practice channel**: everything works, but uploads are only simulated on your computer.
   3. **Channel setup**: name, description, country, audience, style, colours, logo.
   4. **Content niche**: pick *one* focused topic per channel (e.g. Cricket). Children's niches switch on "Made for Kids" automatically.
   5. **Languages**: script, voice and subtitle languages.
   6. **Video format**: Short length, long-video length, visual style, voice, music, captions.
   7. **Publishing**: how many videos, what times, your timezone. Keep **Require my approval** on.
   8. **Budget**: Free mode never spends money. In other modes, set a monthly limit and the studio pauses paid work when it is reached.
   9. **AI providers**: keep the suggestions, or paste an API key for better scripts.
   10. **Review**: check everything, tick the confirmation and finish. Leave automation off until you've made a few videos by hand.

## Making a video
1. **Trend Discovery → Scan now**. Topics get a score from 0 to 100. Higher means fresher, faster-growing and a better fit.
2. Click **Approve** on a topic (or **Own topic**), choose Short or Long and **Start production**.
3. In **Content Studio** you can watch each step. With script review on, edit any sentence, on-screen text or picture idea, then click **Approve script & produce**.
4. After rendering, the **Quality check** must pass. Watch the preview.
5. Click **Approve & publish…** and choose *private* (safest), *unlisted*, *public now* or *schedule*.

## Running on autopilot
- The **Start automation** button in the top bar runs trend scans every few hours, collects analytics daily and produces videos up to your daily limits.
- By default the studio only produces topics **you approved**. To let it choose topics itself, enable that in **Settings**.
- Videos still wait for your approval unless you turn off approval for the channel **and** confirm "unattended publishing" in Settings.
- **Pause all** stops new work immediately. Already-approved uploads still go out.

## Good practice for monetisation
- Fewer, better videos beat many similar ones. YouTube demonetises repetitive, mass-produced content.
- Review scripts for accuracy, especially news and sports.
- Keep the AI disclosure on. Never buy views or subscribers.
- Monetisation thresholds are shown on the dashboard. They are targets, not promises.
